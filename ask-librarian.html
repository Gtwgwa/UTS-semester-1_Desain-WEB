Ask a Librarian

Quick answers

How long can I keep a book?

Seven days. If nobody is waiting for it, ask us about an extension.

How many books can I borrow?

Up to 3 at a time.

What if I return a book late?

The fee is Rp 1.000 per day, and you can pay it when you return the book.

A book is already borrowed. Now what?

Join the waiting list on its page, and we will tell you when it is back.

When can I pick up or return books?

Monday to Friday, 09:00 – 20:00. We are closed on Saturday and Sunday.
